Source health

One row per configured source. fetched is what the source returned, kept is what survived that source's regex filters, shown is what reached the dashboard after its display cap, and at cap means fetched reached catalogueMax — the source stopped because of the configuration, not because it ran out, so it is hiding more than it shows. A row with kept far below fetched is a filter doing its job, not a fault.

Data quality

What share of the corpus each signal actually covers. This is the section that exists because a defect affecting 43% of items sat unnoticed: heat was being computed from terms most items did not carry.

Content signal

What actually arrived, by the day this radar first saw it — not by the publisher's own date, which means a different thing in every feed.

Demand signal

Not collected. Which verticals and technologies readers look at would say which industries are asking for what — the one thing here with commercial value. It is deliberately empty: personalisation is computed in the reader's own browser and never transmitted, and nothing aggregated exists to show. Turning this on is a decision with a disclosure and a consent attached, not a default.
